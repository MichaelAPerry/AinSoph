## Ain Soph 0.1.0 — first public alpha

A persistent world whose people — and whose gods — run on your own computer. Nothing phones home.

![Ain Soph demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MichaelAPerry/AinSoph/main/docs/demo/tour.gif)

### Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `AinSoph-Setup-0.1.0.exe` — installs per user (no administrator prompt), adds Start-menu and desktop shortcuts, uninstalls from Add/Remove Programs. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer is not yet code-signed: choose **More info → Run anyway**. |
| **Linux** | `AinSoph-0.1.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` — extract and run `linux/AinSoph.x86_64`. |

Needs a 64-bit x86 CPU, 8 GB of RAM and about 1.5 GB of disk. **No graphics card needed** — the AI runs on the CPU. The AI model (Qwen 2.5 1.5B Instruct, Apache 2.0) is included.

### Know before you play

- **The world does not pause.** Hunger and sleep run on real hours. Logging out is sleeping where you stand — stay away more than a day without eating and your character will have starved. Shelter in a cave.
- NPCs think on your CPU, so a reply takes a few seconds on a modest machine.
- This is an alpha. Saves may not carry over to later versions.

### In this release

- An endless world of biome regions — wilderness, desert, rivers, sea, forest, grove, mountains, valleys — generated the same way every time for a seed.
- Real-time survival: eat once a day, sleep 8 hours, only caves are safe.
- Four founding travellers in every new world; NPCs who think, talk in character, remember, and take what they make to the Council.
- 30 animal species from Deuteronomy 14 and Leviticus 11 — clean ones are food; lions, wolves, bears and eagles hunt.
- The Triune Council: pray at the hidden altar and three seats vote in parable on what you ask to bring into the world. Approved rules become laws every NPC lives by.
- The rib: after a week of play, name and describe your spouse; children follow each week, carrying your lineage.
- Routes: send travellers to a friend's world and receive theirs.
- Music, ambience and sound, an Esc menu with settings, first-time hints, and an output filter on everything the AI says.
